The Delivery Cycle

PLAN, SPRINT, REVIEW & ITERATE

01

Discovery & Planning

Backlog grooming, user story mapping, sprint goal definition and architecture decisions.

02

Design & Architecture

UX/UI wireframes, system design, database schema and technical blueprint per sprint.

03

Agile Sprints

Iterative two-week sprints with continuous integration, small reviewable pull requests and daily standups.

04

Testing & QA

Automated unit, integration and regression testing with sprint review demos and stakeholder sign-off.

05

Deployment

CI/CD pipelines, staged rollout to production and monitored release with zero-downtime delivery.

06

Retrospect & Iterate

Sprint retrospective, performance monitoring, user feedback loops and continuous improvement cycles.
