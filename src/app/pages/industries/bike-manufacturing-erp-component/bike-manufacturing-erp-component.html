THE PROBLEM

Challenges in Bike Manufacturing

Bike manufacturers face unique operational complexities. Our ERP is designed to eliminate them at every layer of your business.

01 — 04
BOM

Complex Bill of Materials

Hundreds of components per model variant — frames, drivetrains, brakes, electronics — each with multiple suppliers and lead times.

  • • Multi-level BOM management
  • • Variant configuration
  • • Component substitution rules
SCM

Supply Chain Volatility

Fluctuating raw material costs, import dependencies, and vendor reliability issues disrupt production schedules and increase costs.

  • • Multi-vendor procurement
  • • Lead time forecasting
  • • Supplier performance scoring
QC

Quality Compliance

Maintaining consistent safety standards across high-volume production runs while meeting regulatory certifications is a persistent burden.

  • • Inline quality checkpoints
  • • Defect tracking & rework flows
  • • Certification management
DIST

Dealer Management

Managing dealer orders, inventory allocation, warranty claims, and after-sales service across a wide distribution network is fragmented.

  • • Dealer portal & order management
  • • Warranty & service tracking
  • • Regional inventory allocation
PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

Core ERP Modules

Eight integrated modules that cover every dimension of bike manufacturing operations — from shop floor to showroom.

PROD
Production Planning

Schedule production runs, manage work orders, track shop floor progress and monitor assembly line efficiency in real time.

INV
Inventory Management

Real-time stock tracking across warehouses, automatic reorder points, batch/serial number traceability and cycle counting.

PROC
Procurement

Automate purchase orders, vendor quotations, goods receipt and invoice matching with full audit trails.

QC
Quality Control

Define inspection plans, record quality results at each production gate, manage non-conformances and drive corrective actions.

SALES
Sales & CRM

Manage dealer orders, customer enquiries, quotations, order confirmations and delivery schedules from one dashboard.

DEAL
Dealer Management

Dealer portal with stock visibility, warranty claim submission, spare parts ordering and territory-based performance analytics.

FIN
Finance & Accounting

General ledger, accounts payable/receivable, cost center analysis, budget tracking and tax-compliant financial reporting.

HR
HR & Payroll

Attendance management, shift scheduling for factory workers, automated payroll processing and compliance reporting.
